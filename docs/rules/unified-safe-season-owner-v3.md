## Actual single-owner two-phase Tick

The sole WorldTick first advances the ordinary city and completes existing production, forge, technique and PK jobs. It then invokes safe season PreIncome exactly once with the actual current city food, unique TP, current thirty-slot facilities and current resident skill holders. Resident means not currently deployed, traveling or away; reservations and same-turn action use are not invented as passive-skill suppression. Current PK catalog `feng-shui`/`prayer` keys (mapped to the helper DTO holder names `fengshui`/`prayer`) come from the unique PK people metadata, including a legitimate completion earlier in this Tick. Travel arrival and field recovery happen later, so their new residency does not retroactively affect the season phase. This cross-service sampling order is an explicit composition policy, not a claim of recovered native order.

On non-month-start turns PreIncome only advances its lastDate; no monthly counter/draw/post phase is run. On day1 it performs the sourced decay→activate→locust phases. Food and TP patches must match their current before-values, then are applied to the same city/progress owners. The returned farm removals become the season's exact slot+ID tombstones. The existing single income writer then reads the post-removal current facility view and the post-migration harvest boolean, credits gold/food once, and only after that succeeds does PostIncome generate future safe events. Existing payroll/natural loyalty, city/transport/field food, travel and field fire/status phases then continue normally.

Any later failure—including insufficient TP during a selected farm removal, income/capacity, wages causing forbidden zero loyalty, travel, fire, status recovery, date or serialization budget—rejects the entire outer command. There is no partially committed RNG prefix, deleted farm, income payment or clock advance. The pure helper may return failed-attempt evidence, but that is not a committed world state. There is no extra public season-advance or repeat-generation command.
